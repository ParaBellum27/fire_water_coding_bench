PUBLIC CORRECTNESS REQUIREMENTS
BOOT: Run directly from a basic static HTTP server in Chromium. Fit a 1280x960 viewport and adapt to other sizes without changing logical geometry. Include visible gem progress, loss/win feedback, a Restart button, and controls.
LAYOUT: Preserve the multi-tier temple, bottom-left spawns, elemental pools, central vertical moving barrier, four pressure plates, upper moving bridge/platform, two suspended tilting platforms over green pools, 16 diamonds, and two matching exits at the upper left. Players must progress through the actual level, not an unrelated arena.
CONTROLS: Fireboy uses ArrowLeft/ArrowRight/ArrowUp. Watergirl uses physical A/D/W keys with normal lowercase typing as well as Shift/Caps Lock. Each set controls only its character. Both characters can move/jump simultaneously. Releasing one character's key must not release the other's. Prevent page scrolling. R restarts.
PHYSICS: Simulate gravity, grounded jumping, an upward-and-downward jump arc, landing, and solid floor/wall/ceiling collision. Standing on terrain or a platform must support the character. Neither character may float in empty space, fly upward indefinitely, pass through solids, or follow an automatic route. Moving and tilted platforms must use collision geometry consistent with their rendered surfaces.
HAZARDS: Lava/red pools are safe and traversable for Fireboy, fatal to Watergirl. Water/blue pools are safe and traversable for Watergirl, fatal to Fireboy. Green pools are fatal to both. Death of either character ends the attempt visibly; dead characters cannot move onward or satisfy victory. Restart remains available.
DIAMONDS: Include eight red diamonds for Fireboy and eight blue for Watergirl at the pictured locations. Only the matching character collects each diamond. A pickup disappears, updates visible progress once, and cannot be counted twice. All 16 diamonds are optional for exit completion; they are tracked separately.
PRESSURE PLATES: The two lower plates, on opposite sides of the central barrier, share control of that barrier. Either occupied lower plate raises/opens it; neither occupied returns it toward the blocking position. The two upper plates share control of the upper bridge/platform. Either occupied upper plate moves it into the bridging position; neither occupied returns it toward rest. Occupancy by either character is sufficient. These are held controls, not permanent toggles. Motion must actually alter traversable collision geometry; contact, not elapsed time, activates the plates.
HANGING PLATFORMS: Both pictured suspended platforms must support characters and respond to where their weight/contact is applied with physical tilt/rotation. Characters can land on, walk on, and jump off them. A fixed horizontal invisible floor under a rotating drawing, or an animation independent of contact, is incorrect. No particular physics library, precise angle limit, or settling time is required.
EXITS: Fireboy must occupy his red exit and Watergirl her blue exit. One matching exit alone, a wrong-character exit, or death is not team completion. Show victory only when both living characters reach their respective exits. Gem totals do not replace the exit condition.
RESET: Both R and the Restart button restore spawns, diamonds/counters, timer, plates, moving/tilting platforms, loss/win state, and held-input state after progress, death, or completion. Old timers/callbacks must not affect the new attempt.
NO PLAYBACK: With no player input, characters may settle under gravity but must never traverse the route or win. Never declare victory from elapsed time, screenshot/frame indexes, scripted positions, a recognized input sequence, or a candidate-written score. Ordinary alternative player routes must remain possible.
